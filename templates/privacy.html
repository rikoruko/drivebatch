Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What DriveBatch does

DriveBatch helps you scan public Google Drive folders and download or package selected media files directly in your browser.

Google Drive access & Zero-Egress

DriveBatch accesses metadata only for files in public Google Drive folders that you explicitly choose to scan. It does not require Google account login, password access, or authorization to private Drive data. All file transfers, packaging, and stream handling take place strictly between your browser and Google Drive servers. No binary file data passes through or resides on our application server.

Data storage

DriveBatch does not store your files, links, or batch job history on any backend server or database. Application state exists only temporarily within your local browser session.

Third-party services

DriveBatch uses Google Drive APIs solely to fetch public folder metadata. Your browser interacts directly with Google Drive services under Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices & Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy or data handling, please contact drivebatch.support@gmail.com.
