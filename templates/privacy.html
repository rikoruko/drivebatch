Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

What DriveBatch does

DriveBatch helps you scan Google Drive folders and download, compress, or save selected media files.

Google Drive access

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Google provides DriveBatch with an access token. The app uses it only to list files you request, download selected files, and upload generated files when you choose “Save to Google Drive.” DriveBatch does not sell your data or use Drive content for advertising.

Data storage

OAuth credentials are stored in your session cookie and temporary job files are stored on the application server while a download or conversion is running. Temporary files and job records are automatically removed after their retention period. Do not upload sensitive material unless you accept this processing.

Third-party services

DriveBatch may use Google Drive and CloudConvert to provide the features you request. Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time using the logout button and revoke access from your Google Account security settings. Contact the site owner listed by the site operator for privacy questions or deletion requests.
