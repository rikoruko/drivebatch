Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

What DriveBatch does

DriveBatch helps you scan public Google Drive folders and download or compress selected media files.

Google Drive access

DriveBatch accesses only files in public Google Drive folders that you choose to scan. It does not request Google account login or private Drive access, and it does not sell your data or use Drive content for advertising.

Data storage

Temporary job files are stored on the application server while a download or conversion is running. Temporary files and job records are automatically removed after their retention period. Do not upload sensitive material unless you accept this processing.

Third-party services

DriveBatch may use Google Drive and CloudConvert to provide the features you request. Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact drivebatch.support@gmail.com.
